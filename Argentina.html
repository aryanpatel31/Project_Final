    Argentina

    Argentina is a country of striking contrasts and deep cultural roots, stretching from the Andes Mountains to the windswept plains of Patagonia. With a population of over 45 million, it’s known for its rich literary and artistic traditions, passion for fútbol, and vibrant urban life—especially in Buenos Aires. Argentina’s lifestyle reflects a blend of European influence and Latin American spirit, shaped by history, geography, and a strong sense of identity. On this page, we’ll take a closer look at life expectancy in Argentina and what shapes it.

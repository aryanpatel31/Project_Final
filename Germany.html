    Germany

    Germany is a country known for its precision, innovation, and rich cultural history, from its medieval castles to modern metropolises like Berlin and Munich. With a population of over 83 million, Germany offers a lifestyle that values efficiency, sustainability, and strong community ties. Its economy is one of the largest in the world, and its people take pride in their contributions to art, science, and industry. On this page, we’ll take a closer look at life expectancy in Germany and what shapes it.

    Alcohol Consumption vs Life Expectancy

    This graph explores how alcohol consumption has affected life expactancy in Germany over time. We can see that alcohol consumption has generally decreased and life expectancy has generally increased from 2000 to 2015.

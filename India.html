    India

    India is a country of incredible diversity, with a rich tapestry of cultures, languages, and traditions that stretch across its vast landscape. Home to over 1.4 billion people, it is the world’s largest democracy and one of the fastest-growing economies. From the bustling streets of Mumbai to the peaceful valleys of the Himalayas, India’s lifestyle reflects a blend of ancient customs and modern innovation. On this page, we’ll take a closer look at life expectancy in India and what shapes it.

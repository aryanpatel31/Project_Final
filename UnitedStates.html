    United States of America

    The United States is a vast and diverse country, known for its cultural influence, economic power, and geographic variety—from bustling cities and sprawling suburbs to quiet rural landscapes. Home to over 330 million people, it’s a nation shaped by innovation, immigration, and individualism. The U.S. lifestyle reflects a broad mix of traditions, habits, and values, making it a fascinating subject when exploring national trends in health, living standards, and overall well-being. On this page, we’ll take a closer look at life expectancy in the U.S.

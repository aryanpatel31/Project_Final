    South Africa

    South Africa is a nation of remarkable diversity, often called the "Rainbow Nation" for its rich mix of cultures, languages, and landscapes. From the iconic Table Mountain to vast savannas and bustling cities like Johannesburg and Cape Town, it is home to over 60 million people. South Africa's lifestyle is shaped by its complex history, vibrant communities, and a dynamic blend of tradition and modernity. On this page, we’ll take a closer look at life expectancy in South Africa and what shapes it.
